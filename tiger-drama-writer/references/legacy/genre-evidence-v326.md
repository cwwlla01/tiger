# V3.26 genre-contract evidence gate

After writing an upgrade-fantasy episode, independently preserve the raw screenplay. Build a JSON manifest with `genre: power_progression` and an `episodes` array. For each episode record `episode` (integer), and five **verbatim script spans**: `ability_setup`, `ability_use`, `obstacle_changed`, `cost_or_limit`, `end_state`. Record a `power_gain` object including `new_points: 0` when there was no point gain; when points were earned, also specify verified `verified_source` and `balance_after`. In the accompanying human ledger, record opening balance and all spending, and note contradictions. Do not fake an actual gain simply to fulfill this tool.

Run `python scripts/check_genre_contract.py script.md evidence.json`; a nonzero exit blocks the **claim of having collected evidence**. A zero exit only means quoted snippets appear in the script and fields are present: it cannot establish that the payoff is genuine, that those snippets refer to the right episode, or that the action is credible. Read the snippets in their actual scene context. Reject an episode when no tactical use affected an obstacle, even if the field was filled with plausible words. For non-upgrade genres, perform an analogous human evidence review, but do not pass another genre through this specialized script.

For a setup/aftermath episode with no capability payoff, set `payoff_deferred: true`, `payoff_debt_due` (later episode integer), and `defer_reason` (8+ characters), while retaining verbatim `cost_or_limit` and `end_state`. The whole tested window needs at least one delivered capability payoff; deferral is not a substitute for it. Verify that the debt really pays in the promised episode. For serial tests, additionally track payoff debt and next-episode injuries, possessions, knowledge and supplies. Do not require new points or spectacular new abilities in every episode: use established capability or real consequences where appropriate. Never interpret structural release gating as complete genre contract verification.


V3.27 correction: each evidence quote is checked within the declared `## EPnn` section. Do not reference a quote from another episode; use separate declarations for multiple scripts, and do not place broad recap quotations as a substitute for the actual scene.
