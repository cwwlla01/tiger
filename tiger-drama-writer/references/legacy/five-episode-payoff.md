# Five episode payoff review

1. Identify the promise created by previous 1–3 episodes, the exact episode it comes due, and whether the viewer sees it fulfilled.
2. Inspect each episode's scene-level **protagonist decision** and resulting changed state, recording earned gains and costs.
3. Do not let protagonist passively endure captures, accusations and threats for more than two episodes without meaningful tactical or emotional payoff.
4. Payoffs must be dramatized, not summarized. Capture/defeat cannot imply faction control: demonstrate actual transfer of guards, food, keys, inventory, access and buy-in; remaining dissent must be marked.
5. For fantastical powers, keep explicit previous balance + acquired - allocated = remaining and carry wounds/toxic exposures forward. If upstream stat records are absent, label the pending reconciliation.
6. Audit villains' intelligence: why do they permit the move, what do they believe, which weaknesses are already foreshadowed? Audit spatial mechanics of traps in text.
7. Provide a clear episode completion label and distinguish scene scripts from timed shooting-ready texts; word count alone is not runtime verification.
8. Record user canon as LOCKED_USER, assumptions as PROPOSED. Never imply the user's future mysteries are fully resolved by a local victory.
