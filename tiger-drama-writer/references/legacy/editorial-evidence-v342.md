# V3.42 Character voice, emotional payoff and runtime evidence review

After structural release gating, run `python scripts/editorial_probe.py SCRIPT.md --output editorial_probe.json`. This is **descriptive diagnostic output, never a quality or duration certification**.

## Character blind read
For each central character, sample six or more lines from across at least two scenes. Remove names, randomize order, and ask whether speech reveals goals, fear, diction or impulse. If their lines are interchangeable, revise *tactics and subtext* in an independent copy; adding verbal tics is insufficient. The generated per-speaker line statistics are only prompts to human review, not a pass/fail score.

## Emotional payoff evidence
For each episode cite (1) a concrete promise at risk, (2) an action with irreversible cost, (3) an affected witness, (4) a changed relationship or decision visible on-page, (5) evidence that the next episode respects the consequence. Reject 'characters discuss a feeling' as proof of earned emotion. Keep original first drafts immutable.

## Runtime humility
The estimate divides spoken Han-character counts by an assumed rate of 200–320/min and adds an illustrative 4–20 seconds per scene. These values are *uncalibrated planning assumptions*, not measured acting speed; action, reaction, pauses, performance and edit decisions are unknown. The report must say `timed_readthrough=false`; a timed rehearsed read or recorded staging is necessary for duration acceptance. Never quote the estimated range as verified actual runtime.

## Release contract
Structural, scene-depth and batch checks are necessary but insufficient; editorial voice/payoff review and actual timed read-through are independent open gates. Do not turn an editorial check-list or a length estimate into automatic production approval.
