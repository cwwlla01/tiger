# Replacement-scene audit

For revisions to a prior serialized episode span, preserve the prior scene/event outcomes, state which replaced scenes differ, and check:

1. The new resource has a plausible physical owner, transport, encounter and acquisition. Mark AI-created lore as a proposal.
2. The power ledger lists the event's gain, allocation, closing balance and what earlier balance remains unknown. Do not force unknown totals.
3. At least two different on-page outcomes illustrate the new ability: immediate and delayed, with a limitation.
4. Character guilt, injuries, alliances and knowledge survive the change; avoid instant loyalty.
5. Mark the episode as 'key-scene rewrite' or 'full episode screenplay' based on actual coverage, and separate editorial estimates from timed readings.
6. Confirm any downstream scenes invalidated by the rewrite before calling it canonical.
