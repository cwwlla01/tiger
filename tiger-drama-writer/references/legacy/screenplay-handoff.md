# Screenplay handoff contract

The output of this skill is a **screenplay package**, never a shot plan. Deliver these items in order:
1. Project title, genre, tone, planned episode count and target reading duration (estimates labeled).
2. Logline and concise story bible: character identities, motives, stakes, end resolution.
3. Per-episode causal spine, with planned vs scripted clearly separated.
4. Full scripts with `EPxx / SCxx`, `INT./EXT. LOCATION — TIME`, concise observable dramatic actions, speaker name, complete speakable dialogue, and the end-of-episode turn.
5. Canon and continuity ledger: who knows what, current relationships, props, revealed and unresolved secrets.
6. Text-only quality gate outcome and unresolved defects.

**Handoff rule:** provide the package to a separate director/video production skill. Never include camera instructions, shot prompts, model-specific video parameters, or rendering schedules. Timing is a reading estimate only unless independently measured.
