# Scale-aware narrative architecture

For 8-20 episodes: one central dilemma, 2-3 important character arcs, minimal setting bible, fully resolved local conflict. For 21-40: 3-5 event arcs, one significant counterforce, continuity map. For 41-100+: structured timeline, bounded power economy, faction incentives, 6-12 event arcs, character change per arc, location/resource ledger, long-tail mystery track and season-specific resolution. These are templates, not compulsory episode counts.

Each 5-10 episode arc: (A) opening disruption, (B) protagonist pursuit, (C) costly obstacle/opponent adaptation, (D) tactical win or loss, (E) irreversible state change, (F) next necessity. Every episode must carry at least one active choice and concrete consequence. Rotate payoffs: survival, competence, reversal, discovery, loyalty, sacrifice, power jump, institutional win, moral conflict. A season finale resolves its *local* major threat while preserving credible unresolved larger world mysteries.

Do not pad by subdividing a single encounter into five episodes; if event inventory is insufficient, invent causal obstacles and secondary agendas rather than repeated fights. Separate world facts necessary for today's episode from private mythos.
