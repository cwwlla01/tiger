# Repair example: evidence-only scene

**Before (weak)**: EP04, 80 seconds. Two characters read a new audit document, describe how it proves the antagonist lied, exchange accusations, and promise to report it. No decision changes access, safety, or relationships. The episode ends with a new anonymous email.

**Audit**: D-blocker no irreversible state change; E-major exposition without visible payoff; V-major the scene depends on readable paragraphs on-screen; D-major repeats a document reveal seen earlier.

**After (scene design; illustrative, not a complete screenplay)**: An engineer is asked to sign shipment release in a workshop. The protagonist notices the safety seal on a real part differs from the locked sample and must choose: expose her confidential access and stop shipping, or stay undercover. She publicly signs the hold order, loses access to the audit floor, and hears a worker thank her because his brother was injured by a similar part. The antagonist now knows her identity. End hook: the worker recognizes the distinct mark on the replacement seal as belonging to the protagonist's former mentor.

**Why stronger**: Observable prop and signature, meaningful decision with cost, altered access, relationship stakes, new question tied to setup. **Still validate** whether she has legitimate authority to stop shipment and whether the mentor connection is seeded; do not solve realism by inventing it.
