# Delivery templates

## One-click season package
1. Creative assumptions (short; only what user did not specify).
2. Title, one-line hook, 150-300 word synopsis, genre promise, central dramatic question, ending resolution.
3. Character cards with goals, misbeliefs, unique speaking style and visible signatures.
4. Arc map with escalating stakes and payoff gates.
5. Episode grid: ep ID, objective/conflict, midpoint shift, episode payoff, cliffhanger, state delta. All requested episodes should be represented.
6. **Full scripts** for each completed episode. Do not call an outline a full screenplay.
7. Continuity/foreshadowing ledger and short production complexity audit.

## Scene format
EP01 / Scene 01 | INT/EXT | Location ID | DAY/NIGHT | Estimated seconds
Cast: ... | Key props: ...
ACTION: Filmable short present-tense movement and visual evidence.
CHARACTER: Natural playable dialogue. Stage business when essential.
TURN: What concretely changed in power, knowledge, relationship, or choice (optional as writer margin note, not spoken).

## Episodes and completeness
Aim for actual runtime via dialogue and playable action rather than nominal word counts. For a long season exceeding available output, deliver complete episode grid plus as many fully scripted contiguous episodes as can fit. Label clearly 'Episodes written in full' and 'Episodes planned but not scripted'. Export to files when supported. Do not fill with placeholders disguised as scenes.
