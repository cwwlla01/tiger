# Voice-through-choice / emotional payoff diagnostic (V3.62)

## Failure observed
In the EP04 hospital security scene, three speakers could all plausibly deliver the same measured analysis of forms, evidence and protocol. The registration result was causal, but the sibling trust shift was primarily stated rather than enacted.

## Rewrite procedure
1. Freeze facts: a stranger's image of the hospital door does not identify the stalker; police can request footage, siblings cannot seize it; the mother is still under observation; the photograph original is elsewhere.
2. For every speaking character write a pressure tactic: 周宁 insists on accountable disclosure; 周尧 first suspects systems and then offers personal liability; 保卫员 refuses unlawful handover but can arrange safe waiting.
3. Replace one abstract explanation with a visible obstacle and irreversible choice: ask for an identity and a callable phone number; 周尧 voluntarily signs in after initially resisting and leaves his phone to be contacted.
4. Carry a relational consequence: she lets him take the next difficult conversation; he must actually follow through, not merely promise.
5. Do not merely add an argument or adjectives. At least one practical action should change who bears risk.

## Compact before / after
**Before (generic):** 周尧：登记会泄密。周宁：不登记就没有证据。保卫员：请按程序。

**After (distinct action):** 周尧把保温瓶塞进怀里：别把我号码留给他们。周宁把表推回去：那我一个人签；可警察回电时，你也别装不知道。周尧看着她签下的姓名，伸手把表按住：划掉“同行人员空缺”。写我。保卫员递来笔：只留能接通的号。周尧报出号码，手机当场响起；他接通警方的核实电话，首次承担公开作证的风险。

**Result:** The case is not solved and the stalker is still unknown. The costly voluntary choice changes sibling trust and produces a verifiable contact route.

## Validation
Check every episode's actual on-stage commitment and next-scene consequence. Use the existing static gates for structure, then a separate human voice-blind read and an independent emotional-payoff review. Never claim a sample revision demonstrates stable first-pass performance across genres.
