# V3.15 First-draft screenplay engine (mandatory)

Goal: prevent summarizing a scene plan into two sentences and two lines of dialogue. This file governs the GENERATION pass, not just post-hoc QC.

## Scene embodiment procedure

For every episode: identify the immediate object of conflict, who physically possesses/controls it, what each speaking character wants, what they can do right now, and why the protagonist cannot merely walk away. Draft sequential playable beats, not plot statements.

For each scene, write:
1. Observable initial situation and an immediate, concrete goal.
2. Opponent/environment response that changes the physical situation (not just a threat).
3. A specific attempt by the protagonist, with a failed or costly step if warranted.
4. Responsive dialogue: each exchange seeks an action, refusal, truth, concealment or concession, not exposition for the viewer.
5. An irreversible consequence visible before leaving the scene. Carry the exact state into the next scene.

Do not draft scene prose as 'they negotiate, then the enemy agrees' or 'she fights and wins.' Stage the steps of refusal, leverage, countermeasure, and consequences. Do not pad with repeated arguments, arbitrary reversals or decorative movement. Show competence and counterplay.

## Batch generation commitment

Produce one episode at a time in full script form and check scene embodiment BEFORE starting the next episode. If the desired episode length cannot be met, stop promising batch completeness: deliver the finished subset and explicitly mark the remainder SCENE_DRAFT or UNWRITTEN. Prioritize finished episodes over sparse coverage. A completed preflight is not proof of a completed screenplay.

## Cross-genre behavior

- Power fantasy: tangible earned capability/advantage, limited by meaningful costs; exact power/asset ledger.
- Relationship drama: emotionally specific choices and changed relationship stakes; avoid forced confrontations for a cliffhanger.
- Mystery: evidence chain distinguishes observation from inference, and disclosures change decisions or danger; avoid serial paper clues.

## Honest acceptance

A script qualifies only if there is a full playable chain, not simply scene headings, distinct names, or sufficient characters. A static checker flags risks but does not certify quality or 2–3 minutes. Runtime is UNTIMED unless a real read-through was performed. Report first-pass vs after-rewrite separately. On test, include actual screenplay samples from at least three genres before claiming cross-genre gains; do not infer broad reliability from one sample.
