# Causal regression and repair protocol

1. Select the latest internally consistent *candidate* baseline; compare with locked user canon and keep provenance.
2. For every episode, record `SCENE | objective | action | evidence available | inference | earned payoff | real cost | ending type`.
3. Mark blockers separately from structural screen: **unsupported inference** (e.g. a leaked decoy is insufficient to identify a leaker without controlling the source), **resource creation** (a diverted contaminated stream does not become clean stored water), **knowledge teleportation**, **unpaid promise**, **antagonist convenience**, **repetitive last-image hook**.
4. Demonstrate a falsifiable repair: exact earlier line -> new line/action; update downstream deductions, inventories, injuries and episode hooks. Prefer a physical test, observation, tradeoff or adversarial choice, not explanatory dialogue.
5. Acceptance tiers: `SCREENED` (automated formatting only), `CAUSALLY_REVIEWED` (editorial scene evidence), `TIMED` (performed read-through), `PRODUCTION_CANDIDATE` (all required checks + consistent handoff). Never promote tiers silently. A strong episode may be short; a long episode may be incoherent.
6. Never create new world rules merely to conceal an old contradiction. Proposed new plot details remain proposals.
