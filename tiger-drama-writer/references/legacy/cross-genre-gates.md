# Cross-genre adaptive quality gates (V1.4)

Apply after the universal gates in auto-quality-loop.md. Choose a primary emotional promise and secondary promise; audit payoff type against them. Never force a revenge-status payoff onto a romance or a courtroom-proof payoff onto a suspense plot.

## Common self-test
For each episode, log (1) desired audience emotion, (2) visible trigger, (3) protagonist's costly choice, (4) earned change, (5) what is still withheld, and (6) AI-shot feasibility. Across three adjacent episodes, require variation in the **mechanism** of payoff, not necessarily the emotion. Do not count a cliffhanger alone as a payoff.

## Urban reversal
Promise: catharsis via initiative, unfairness reversed through credible agency. Passing examples: protagonist wins a tangible resource; changes another person's options; voluntarily sacrifices an advantage for moral leverage. Fail when repeated humiliation is the only pressure, when villains repeatedly forget prior losses, when institutional authority appears without setup, or when every win is a document reveal. Progression: personal humiliation → tactical turn → cost/retaliation → moral decision → new arena.

## Romance
Promise: chemistry + changing trust / attachment. Reward is a costly emotional action witnessed by another person, not just the words 'I love you'. Log trust status and knowledge asymmetry for both leads. Fail when the only engine is an easily resolved misunderstanding, non-consensual coercion treated as romance, obligatory kiss as a substitute for decisions, or no relational change across two episodes. Rotate payoffs: care, boundary-respect, vulnerability, sacrifice, renewed mistrust, mutual agency. Favor playable micro-gestures and prop continuity for script.

## Mystery
Promise: fair inquiry and progressive reinterpretation. Maintain a clue table: first appearance, visible location, accessible observer, current interpretation, later payoff. Every major reveal must have prior on-screen support; each episode should resolve one concrete question while opening another. Fail when solutions require an off-screen miracle, detective knows facts not shown, suspect changes arbitrarily, or surveillance/forensics are treated as infallible magic. Rotate deduction, physical discovery, strategic trap, witness choice, ethical dilemma, and danger; do not use endless anonymous messages.

## Additional screenplay gates
Keep dramatic action visible in scene prose, characters intelligible, and prop transfers consistent. Assess text only. Do not plan shots or video renders.

## Cross-genre assessment table
For each test genre, publish: actual scripted episodes vs planned; first-scene hook evidence; primary genre promise achieved?; secondary payoff type; state delta; repeated beat risk; screenplay bottleneck; blocker count; revised passage if blockers or majors. Text-only scores are a diagnostic, not proof of retention.
