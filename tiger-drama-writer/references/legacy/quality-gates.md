# Quality assurance: autonomous script-doctor

Audit at the season, episode and scene levels. Scores are subjective editorial diagnostics (1-5), not validated audience metrics.

1. Premise clarity and distinctive hook: would audience understand the urgent emotional question?
2. Character agency and credible motivation: does protagonist make costly decisions? does opposition adapt?
3. Causal chain: does each major event follow from a choice, established evidence, or credible circumstance?
4. Momentum: does every episode change story state; are conflict and emotional variations non-repetitive?
5. Payoff density: are setups paid, reversals earned, emotional release concrete?
6. Cliffhangers: each has a meaningful unresolved consequence and varied mechanism.
7. Dialogue: distinct voice, subtext, speakability, no exposition speeches or interchangeable lines.
8. Continuity: knowledge, time, wardrobe, relationships, injuries, props and revealed secrets consistent.
9. AI shootability: visible beats, stable assets, manageable actions and staging.
10. Ending: central question resolved, protagonist transformed, planted motifs paid off.

Workflow: identify 3-5 highest-severity defects, rewrite impacted scenes or episode grid, recheck continuity and downstream impacts. For any core dimension scoring 2 or below, revise before delivery; scoring cannot by itself guarantee quality. On exceptionally long outputs, report remaining limitations and unreviewed sections explicitly. Never assert quality certification, virality, or real retention performance.
